# Codebook: `data_survey_psj-subset.csv`

The data used to replicate the tables and figures in Bower-Bir (2022), *Policy Studies Journal* 50(4): 757–795: an unaltered subset of the Economic Justice Survey (version 7, SocialSci, November 2013 to April 2014), 992 respondents by 27 columns (two identifiers and 25 variables). Question wording is quoted from the survey as fielded.

Two conventions:

- **Direction × strength.** A yes-or-no question coded 1 or −1 is multiplied by a follow-up "How strongly do you feel this way?" coded Very strongly 3, Strongly 2, Somewhat strongly 1, Not at all strongly 0. The product runs from −3 to 3.
- **Rescaled to 0–1.** Every scale except `age`, `employment`, `race`, `party`, `state`, and `post_weight` is rescaled to run from 0 to 1 by (x − min) / (max − min), so a −3 to 3 scale becomes (x + 3) / 6. The table gives the raw coding; the file holds the rescaled value.

The second column gives each variable's label as the article prints it. A label with no location in parentheses appears in all four regression tables: Table 1 and appendix Tables C1, C2, and D1.
